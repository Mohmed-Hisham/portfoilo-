Easy Store

Client: Independent Client

Role: Lead Developer

Problem: The client needed a system to track fabric inventory by color and quantity, but was manually recording data in Excel. They also needed a complete financial and customer management system accessible anywhere, not just from the office.

Solution: Built a cross-platform Flutter app (web + mobile) using GetX for state management and Firebase for backend. Implemented a color picker that displays the actual color when typing its name. Integrated full inventory, sales, purchases, accounting, and customer modules.

Impact: The app reduced manual errors by 90%, improved inventory accuracy, and enabled remote access for the business owner. Now used daily in operations.
